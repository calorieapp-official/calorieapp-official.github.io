Support

Questions, problems or ideas — write to us. You can also manage your account and data directly in the app.

Contact us

Write to us by e-mail. Please describe the problem, the app version (shown in Settings → About) and your iOS version. Do not send health details or other sensitive information we do not need.

Write an e-mail calorieapp.support@gmail.com

Delete your account

  1. Open Settings → Account.
  2. Tap Delete account.
  3. Choose whether to turn on “Also delete data on this iPhone”. If it stays off, your data on the iPhone is kept.
  4. Confirm with Apple or Google. An internet connection is required.

If deleting in the app is not possible, write to calorieapp.support@gmail.com and we will delete your account.

Delete all data on your iPhone

  1. Open Settings → Privacy & data.
  2. Tap Delete all my data and confirm.

This deletes everything CalorieApp stores on this iPhone and returns the app to its first launch. It does not delete your account. What happens to data already processed by our backend or AI providers is explained in the Privacy Policy.

Change your AI and analytics choices

Open Settings → Privacy & data to withdraw AI permission, turn usage analytics off or switch automatic weekly reviews off. Changes apply immediately.

Frequently asked questions

Is CalorieApp free?

Yes. The first version is free, with no subscriptions and no advertising. AI features have usage limits to keep the service running.

Do I need an account?

No. Your diary and manual logging work without one, and you get one free AI food recognition. After that, AI features — and the Companion chat and weekly review at any time — need sign-in with Apple or Google.

Where is my data stored?

Your profile, diary, weight and chat history are stored on your iPhone; there is no cloud sync. What you send to an AI feature — meal text, a food photo, a chat message — is processed online to answer it.

Which AI services are used?

Meal text, Companion chat and weekly reviews are processed by OpenAI; meal photos by Google Gemini. Requests go through our own backend, and the app contains no AI provider keys.

Why are the numbers only estimates?

A photo or a short description cannot always reveal exact weights, oils, sauces or hidden ingredients. Always check and correct an entry before you rely on it.

Who can use CalorieApp?

CalorieApp is intended for adults aged 18 and over. It is not a medical product and does not give medical advice.
