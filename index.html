Everything a food diary needs, without the busywork

Built around one loop: log food, see where your day stands, follow your weight, adjust with care.

  • Log food in your own words

    Type what you ate the way you would say it — for example “two eggs and toast with butter”. AI turns it into a meal you can edit before it is saved.

  • Or take a photo

    Take a photo or pick one from your library. AI estimates the foods and portions, and you can add a short note to help.

  • You check every item

    When a food or portion is unclear, CalorieApp asks instead of guessing and never silently swaps in a different product. Totals that include estimates are marked as approximate.

  • Today and progress

    Calories with protein, fat and carbs for the day, weight charts for 7, 30 and 90 days, nutrition summaries and a logging calendar.

  • Your own Companion

    Choose your Companion’s name, look and tone, and chat about your eating with today’s context. Memory notes are saved only when you confirm them.

  • Weekly review

    An optional summary of how your week went. Automatic weekly reviews stay off until you turn them on.

Also included

  • Favorites, recent meals, search and history by day
  • Your own food values and custom dishes
  • Local reminders for meals, weigh-ins and the weekly review — off by default
  • 11 languages
  • Light and dark colour themes

Private by design

Your food diary is personal. CalorieApp is built so that it stays that way.

  • Your diary stays on your iPhone

    Your profile, diary, weight and Companion chat are stored on your device. There is no cloud sync.

  • AI only when you allow it

    The first time you use an AI feature, the app asks. Until you choose Allow, nothing is sent to an AI provider. Manual logging works without AI.

  • No ads, no tracking

    No advertising, no advertising identifiers, and we do not sell your data. Usage analytics are optional and off by default.

  • An account is optional

    Sign in with Apple or Google only when you want more AI. You can delete your account in the app at any time.

Sign in with Google: what we use

Signing in is optional. If you choose Google, CalorieApp receives your email address and Google account ID and, if Google includes them, your name and a link to your profile picture. We use them only to run your account and to apply AI usage limits. Your diary is not uploaded, and your Google account data is not sent to AI providers, used for advertising or sold. You can delete your account in Settings → Account → Delete account.

Estimates, not medical advice

Calories and nutrients are estimates and can be wrong. CalorieApp is not a medical product: talk to a professional before major changes to your diet. CalorieApp is for adults aged 18 and over.
